01 · Introduction

What is Kudi Arc?

A full financial toolkit for Africans — built on the blockchain, designed for simplicity.

"Kudi" means money in Hausa and Yoruba — Nigeria's two most spoken languages. "Arc" is the blockchain we built on. Together: money on Arc.

Kudi Arc is a decentralised application (dApp) built on Arc Network — the first blockchain where gas is paid in USDC instead of ETH. This means African users only need USDC to use the entire platform. No ETH. No complicated setup. Just connect your wallet and go.

We combine three powerful financial tools in one place: FX Swap, Remittance, and Yield. Products that normally require three different apps, multiple KYC processes, and a lot of friction — Kudi Arc puts them all in one clean interface.

04 · How It Works

How Does Kudi Arc Work?

The full journey from connecting your wallet to receiving cash — explained simply.

1
You connect your crypto wallet
Open kudiarc.xyz on MetaMask or Rabby browser. Click "Connect Wallet". Arc Testnet is added automatically — no manual setup needed. Your wallet address is your identity. No email. No password. No KYC.
💡 Works on MetaMask, Rabby, and any WalletConnect-compatible wallet
2
You fund your wallet with USDC
Get testnet USDC for free at faucet.circle.com. On mainnet, you'll send real USDC from any exchange (Binance, Coinbase, etc.) to your wallet. USDC also pays your gas fees on Arc — no ETH needed.
🚰 Testnet is 100% free — experiment without risk
3
You choose what to do
Pick from three options: Swap your stablecoins, Send money to a bank account, or Deposit for yield. Each action is clear, with live rates and a preview of exactly what you'll receive before you confirm.
4
You sign in your wallet — not us
Every transaction requires your cryptographic signature in your wallet. This means nobody — not even Kudi Arc — can move your funds without your approval. Two signatures are required: one to approve the token, one to execute the transaction.
🔐 Your keys, your money — always
5
Transaction confirms on Arc blockchain
Arc confirms transactions in under 1 second with deterministic finality. Every transaction is permanently recorded on the blockchain and verifiable on explorer.testnet.arc.io. You get a reference number for every send.
6
Recipient gets local cash
For Send transactions, our payout partners (Flutterwave, with Bitnob and Paystack as fallbacks) convert the stablecoin to local currency and credit the recipient's bank account or mobile wallet. On testnet payouts are simulated (sandbox); real payouts are planned for mainnet.
🏦 Supports 26 Nigerian banks and mobile money in supported countries
05 · Getting Started

Step-by-Step Guide

From zero to your first transaction in under 5 minutes.

🔧 SETUP (First Time Only)
1
Install MetaMask or Rabby
Download MetaMask (metamask.io) or Rabby (rabby.io) as a browser extension or mobile app. Create a new wallet and save your seed phrase somewhere safe — this is the ONLY way to recover your wallet.
↗ Get MetaMask
2
Open Kudi Arc and connect
Go to kudiarc.xyz. Click the green "Connect" button in the top bar. Your wallet will ask permission to connect — click "Connect". Arc Testnet network is added automatically.
↗ Open kudiarc.xyz
3
Get free testnet USDC
Visit faucet.circle.com, connect your wallet, and claim free USDC on Arc Testnet. This USDC has no real value — it's for testing. You can claim multiple times. You need USDC to pay gas fees too.
↗ Circle Faucet
⇄ MAKE YOUR FIRST SWAP
4
Go to Swap tab
On the Swap page, you'll see the live rate (e.g. 1 USDC = 0.8697 EURC). Enter an amount in the "You Send" field. You'll instantly see how much EURC you'll receive, the fee, and the NGN equivalent.
5
Click Swap and approve in wallet
Click the Swap button. Your wallet will pop up twice: first to approve the USDC spend (Step 1/2), then to execute the swap (Step 2/2). Confirm both. The swap completes in seconds and you'll see your new EURC balance.
📤 SEND MONEY TO A BANK ACCOUNT
6
Go to Send tab
Choose your send token (USDC or EURC), enter the amount, and select the recipient's country. The live exchange rate and NGN/KES/GHS equivalent are shown instantly.
7
Enter recipient bank details
Choose delivery method (Bank Account or Mobile Money). For Nigeria, select the bank from the dropdown (26 banks supported). Enter account number and account name. For other countries, enter mobile number.
8
Review and sign
A confirmation modal shows all details — amount sent, amount received, fee, recipient. Review carefully, then click "Sign & Send". Approve in your wallet. A reference number is generated for tracking.
📈 EARN YIELD WITH USYC
9
Get USYC allowlist approved
USYC is a regulated product. Circle requires one-time wallet approval. DM @KudiArc on X with your wallet address and we'll help get you approved. Takes 24-48 hours.
10
Deposit USDC → receive USYC
On the Yield tab, enter how much USDC to deposit. You'll see the USYC you receive and your estimated yearly earnings. Click Deposit, approve in wallet. Your USYC position grows in value automatically — no claiming needed.
